ChipTrail — Delete Your Account

JaySmitt Labs

You can delete your ChipTrail rider account yourself, right inside the app, at any time. It happens at once and can't be undone.

Delete it in the app

  • Open ChipTrail and tap Friends on the Home screen.
  • Scroll to the very bottom and tap Delete my account.
  • Read what will be deleted, type DELETE and confirm.

Or ask us by email

If you no longer have the app, email support@jaysmittlabs.com with your rider name and friend code (or as much as you remember). We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What gets deleted from our server

  • Your rider name and friend code
  • Your friends and friend requests
  • Your place on every leaderboard, and your Chips by Four games and records
  • Your Apple or Google sign-in link
  • Your backup, including every backed-up chip and profile photo, and your shared profile

What is kept

  • Chips, photos, points and badges stored on your own phone stay there until you delete the app.
  • A scrambled phone tag used only to stop invite points being claimed twice. It has no link to the deleted account and can't be turned back into anything about you.
  • Support emails, only as long as needed to answer you and no longer than 24 months.
