How we work
Small on purpose. It's the only way the details survive.
01

We use it first

Every product here started as something one of us needed at work or at home. It has to earn its place in our own day before it goes anywhere near a store.

02

You buy it, you own it

One payment, or free. No subscriptions, no licence that expires, nothing that stops working because a renewal lapsed. Updates come with it.

03

Support is a person

Write to us and one of the two people who built the thing writes back. There is no tier one, and there is no queue to escalate out of.

What that means in practice
  • No subscriptions on anything we sell
  • Free updates for as long as we're building it
  • Our games can be played free, start to finish
  • Nothing you paid for is lost by reinstalling
  • A refund policy in plain English
  • Email answered by the builder, not a bot
