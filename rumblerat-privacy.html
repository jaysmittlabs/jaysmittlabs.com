RumbleRat — Privacy Policy

JaySmitt Labs

The short version: Your game — coins, gems, rats, motorcycles, upgrades, trophies and stats — lives on your phone. If you choose a nickname to play online, RumbleRat keeps your nickname, profile card, race numbers and race recordings on our server so you can add friends, race their ghosts and appear on the standings. RumbleRat never uses your location, camera, photos, microphone or contacts. The one outside service that collects data about you is Google AdMob, which shows the ads.

This privacy policy applies to the RumbleRat app for mobile devices (iOS and Android), together with any related services operated by JaySmitt Labs (collectively, the "Application"). JaySmitt Labs is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".

On Your Phone Only

Until you pick a nickname, everything in RumbleRat stays on your own device: your coins and gems, the rats and motorcycles you own and their upgrades, your trophies, Daily Rumble and Weekly Rumble progress, race records and lifetime stats, and your settings. It is stored with the operating system's standard app storage and deleting the Application deletes it. Nothing is sent before you pick a nickname, except what the ads need (see below).

Playing Online: Your Nickname

Friends, Standings, ghost races, Race the World and Chips by Four are optional. If you join, you pick a nickname and RumbleRat creates an account on our server with:

  • Your nickname and a friend code, which other riders can use to add you
  • A random account number and a secret key made by your phone. We keep only a scrambled (hashed) copy of the key, so it cannot be read back
  • Whether you play on iPhone or Android
  • Who you are friends with, and friend requests you have sent or received
  • The last time your phone contacted the server

Nicknames are checked against a list of offensive words, both on your phone and on our server.

Your Profile Card

Your profile card shows your nickname, the title you have earned, one of RumbleRat's own 20 rider pictures (you cannot upload a photo), an optional rider number, an optional country flag, your favourite rat, motorcycle and track, and a few game numbers (trophies, wins, races and longest wheelie). Other riders see your card on the standings, in friends lists and in Chips by Four.

Standings, Ghost Races and Race the World

Standings. After each finished race your phone sends your game totals — for example trophies, races won and finished, coins and gems earned, flips, longest wheelie, riders passed and best time — so you can be ranked against your friends and everyone else who has joined. The standings show your nickname, picture and numbers.

Race recordings. To let friends race your "ghost", your phone sends a recording of your own bike's run on a track (where your bike was on the game's road, its speed, jumps and flips, ten times a second) together with your time, the rat and motorcycle you rode and the track. These are game positions only, never where you or your phone are. We keep your best run on each track, runs sent as challenges to friends, and your runs on each day's Race the World course, which other riders may race against.

Online now. While RumbleRat is open, your phone lets our server know about once a minute, so your friends see a small green dot by your name. The server keeps just the time you were last seen.

Chips by Four. Games with friends, and with riders you don't know through Find a rider, are kept on our server while they are played. A rider you are matched with sees your nickname and picture. You can block a rider, so you are never matched again, and report a nickname that breaks the rules; reports are read by us and used only to rename or remove offensive nicknames.

Advertising

RumbleRat shows ads supplied by Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the screen while racing, a video ad after some races, and optional videos you can choose to watch for free gems. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens. Buying Remove Ads turns off the banner and the video ads. To serve and measure ads, Google collects and receives:

  • Your device's IP address, used to estimate your general area and language
  • An advertising identifier — the Android advertising ID and app set ID on Android, or the Apple identifier for advertisers on iOS only if you allow tracking when your iPhone asks
  • Your interactions with the ads, such as an ad being shown, tapped or a video finished
  • Diagnostic and performance information about the advertising software, used to detect faults and fraud

On iPhone you are asked whether to allow tracking; if you say no, Google cannot use the Apple advertising identifier and ads are less personalised. This information is collected by Google and handled under Google's privacy policy for partner sites and apps. The Service Provider sees only totals, such as how many ads were shown. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for your choices first.

The Application contains no analytics software and no crash-reporting software. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with data brokers.

Where Our Server Runs

RumbleRat's server and database run on Cloudflare, which processes this data for us under its own security and privacy commitments. Connections between the Application and the server are encrypted.

Purchases

Gem bundles and Remove Ads are bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play, which process all payments. The Service Provider never sees or stores your payment card details, billing address or app store account credentials. Your phone keeps a note of each purchase so it is only given once.

Emails You Send Us

If you email us for help, we will have whatever you write, and we use it only to answer you.

Children

RumbleRat is made for teens and adults. It is not directed to children under 13 years of age (or such higher age as required by applicable law), and the Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us at support@jaysmittlabs.com so that it can be deleted.

Your Rights and Deleting Your Account

To delete your online account, open Friends from the Home screen and tap Delete my account at the bottom. That deletes your nickname, friend code, friends, profile card, standings numbers, race recordings, challenges, Race the World times, Chips by Four games, blocks and reports from our server at once and for good; the game on your phone stays. See Delete your RumbleRat account. You can also ask us by email at support@jaysmittlabs.com. Data kept only on your phone is deleted by deleting the Application.

Your California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete it, the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. The Service Provider does not sell personal information. To exercise your rights, contact support@jaysmittlabs.com.

International Data Transfers

Cloudflare and Google may process data in countries outside your country of residence, under the safeguards described in their own policies.

Data Retention

  • On your phone: until you delete the Application.
  • Online account, friends, profile, standings and race recordings: for as long as your account exists, or until you ask us to delete them. Race the World times are removed after a few days.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google under its own policies.
  • Support emails: only as long as needed to resolve your question, and no longer than 24 months.

Security

Your account key is stored on your phone and only a hashed copy is kept on our server. All connections are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, and your device's own lock screen remains the main protection for what is on your phone.

Data Breach Notification

If a data breach occurs that affects personal data for which the Service Provider is responsible, we will notify affected users in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

Changes

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time and will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy here with a new effective date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about privacy while using the Application, please contact the Service Provider by email at support@jaysmittlabs.com.
