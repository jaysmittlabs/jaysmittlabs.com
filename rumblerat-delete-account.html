RumbleRat — Delete Your Account

JaySmitt Labs

You can delete your RumbleRat online account yourself, right inside the app, at any time. It happens at once and can't be undone.

Delete it in the app

  • Open RumbleRat and tap Friends on the Home screen.
  • Scroll to the very bottom and tap Delete my account.
  • Read what will be deleted, type DELETE and confirm.

Or ask us by email

If you no longer have the app, email support@jaysmittlabs.com with your nickname and friend code (or as much as you remember), and we will delete the account for you.

What gets deleted from our server

  • Your nickname, friend code and profile card
  • Your friends and friend requests
  • Your place on every standings list
  • Your race recordings (ghosts), challenges and Race the World times
  • Your Chips by Four games, and any blocks and reports you made or that were made about you

What is kept

  • Coins, gems, rats, motorcycles, upgrades and trophies stored on your own phone stay there until you delete the app.
  • Purchases stay with your Apple or Google account and can be restored with Restore Purchases.
  • Support emails, only as long as needed to answer you and no longer than 24 months.
