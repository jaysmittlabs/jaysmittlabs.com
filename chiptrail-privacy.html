ChipTrail — Privacy Policy

JaySmitt Labs

The short version: Your chip collection lives on your phone. If you choose to join Friends, ChipTrail keeps a backup of your collection on our server so you can get it back on a new phone, and shares only the profile details you mark Friends or Global. We use your location on your phone to check you in at dealerships; we never sell your information or use it to track you. The one outside service that collects data about you is Google AdMob, which plays the optional videos you can watch for a free point.

This privacy policy applies to the ChipTrail app for mobile devices (iOS and Android), together with any related services operated by JaySmitt Labs (collectively, the "Application"). JaySmitt Labs is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".

On Your Phone Only

Until you join Friends, everything you put into ChipTrail stays on your own device: the chips you log and their photos, colours, dates and notes; your check-in history; your points and badges; and your profile. It is stored with the operating system's standard app storage and deleting the Application deletes it.

Reading the words on a chip photo (the Seek feature) happens entirely on your device. The photo is not uploaded to do this.

Joining Friends: Your Rider Account

Friends, Ranks and Backup are optional. If you join, you pick a rider name and ChipTrail creates an account on our server with:

  • Your rider name and a friend code, which other riders can use to add you
  • A random account number and a secret key made by your phone. We keep only a scrambled (hashed) copy of the key, so it cannot be read back
  • Who you are friends with, and friend requests you have sent or received
  • The last time your phone contacted the server

If you choose Sign in with Apple (or Sign in with Google, where offered) to keep your rider safe, we store only the permanent, app-specific account number Apple or Google gives us, so that you can find your rider again on a new phone. ChipTrail does not ask Apple or Google for your name or email address.

What Your Friends and Other Riders See

Every line of your profile — name, city, state, email, phone, bike, bio and photos — has its own setting: Private, Friends or Global. Anything marked Friends or Global is sent to our server and shown to the riders you have added as friends. Anything marked Private is not shared with anyone. Your friends also see your chip, state and badge counts, your badges and the title you wear.

Ranks. To place you on the leaderboards, your phone sends the list of dealerships you have a chip from and the dealerships you have checked in at (as dealership numbers from ChipTrail's own list, not map positions). Global and dealership leaderboards show your rider name and your counts to everyone who has joined. Friends leaderboards show them to your friends.

Backup

Once you have joined Friends, ChipTrail automatically keeps a backup of your collection on our server so you can bring it back on a new phone. The backup contains everything on the phone: your chips and their notes, your chip and profile photos, your check-in history (including where your phone said it was when you checked in), your points, badges and title, and your whole profile — including the lines you marked Private. Only you can ever get your backup back; it is never shown to friends or other riders. Each new backup replaces the last one, and photos you delete are removed from it.

Location

ChipTrail asks to use your location only while you are using the app. It is used on your phone to check you in when you are within about 100 feet of a dealership, to show where you are on the trail map, and to plan a trip from where you are. Your location is not tracked in the background and is not sent to us, except that each check-in record (the dealership, the day and time, and where your phone said it was) is included in your backup if you have joined Friends.

Camera and Photos

ChipTrail uses the camera, or a photo you choose from your library, to picture your chips, your rides and yourself. Photos stay on your phone unless you have joined Friends, in which case they are included in your backup, and your profile photos are shown to friends if your Photos setting allows it.

Maps and Directions

To draw the map, find places you type and draw the road for a trip, ChipTrail uses mapping services. On iPhone these are Apple's own Maps services. On Android they are OpenStreetMap map tiles, the Photon place search (komoot) and the OSRM route service. These services receive what they need to answer — the area of the map being shown, the words you type into the trip box, and the start and end points of a route — and handle it under their own policies. When you send a trip to Apple Maps or Google Maps, that app takes over from there.

Optional Videos (Advertising)

Market offers one optional video a day that you can watch for a free point. These videos are supplied by Google AdMob; ChipTrail shows no other ads and nothing ever interrupts you. ChipTrail asks Google for non-personalised ads only. To serve and measure the videos, Google still collects and receives:

  • Your device's IP address, used to estimate your general area and language
  • An advertising identifier — the Android advertising ID and app set ID on Android, or the Apple identifier for advertisers on iOS where you have allowed it
  • Your interactions with the videos, such as a video being shown or finished
  • Diagnostic and performance information about the advertising software, used to detect faults and fraud

This information is collected by Google and handled under Google's privacy policy for partner sites and apps. The Service Provider sees only totals, such as how many videos were watched. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for your choices first, and you can change them later from Privacy choices in the app's Settings.

The Application contains no analytics software and no crash-reporting software. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with data brokers.

Where Our Server Runs

ChipTrail's server, database and photo storage run on Cloudflare, which processes this data for us under its own security and privacy commitments. Connections between the Application and the server are encrypted.

Purchases

Where the Application offers purchases, all payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. The Service Provider never sees or stores your payment card details, billing address or app store account credentials.

Emails You Send Us

If you email us — for example with Report a change on a dealership page, or to ask for help — we will have whatever you write, and we use it only to answer you and to correct the dealership list.

Children

ChipTrail is a general-audience app for motorcycle riders and collectors. It is not directed to children under 13 years of age (or such higher age as required by applicable law), and the Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us at support@jaysmittlabs.com so that it can be deleted.

Your Rights and Deleting Your Account

You can change any profile line's setting to Private at any time, remove friends, and stop sharing photos. To delete your rider account, open Settings → Friends and tap Delete my account. That deletes your rider account, friend links, leaderboard entries, sign-in link, backup and backed-up photos from our server at once and for good; the chips on your phone stay. You can also ask us by email at support@jaysmittlabs.com. Data kept only on your phone is deleted by deleting the Application.

Your California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete it, the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. The Service Provider does not sell personal information. To exercise your rights, contact support@jaysmittlabs.com.

International Data Transfers

Cloudflare and Google may process data in countries outside your country of residence, under the safeguards described in their own policies.

Data Retention

  • On your phone: until you delete the Application.
  • Rider account, friends, leaderboards and backup: for as long as your rider account exists, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google under its own policies.
  • Support emails: only as long as needed to resolve your question, and no longer than 24 months.

Security

Your account key is stored on your phone and only a hashed copy is kept on our server. A backup can be read back only by the rider it belongs to, and a friend's shared profile and photos only by that rider's friends. All connections are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, and your device's own lock screen remains the main protection for what is on your phone.

Data Breach Notification

If a data breach occurs that affects personal data for which the Service Provider is responsible, we will notify affected users in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

Changes

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time and will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy here with a new effective date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about privacy while using the Application, please contact the Service Provider by email at support@jaysmittlabs.com.
